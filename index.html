What's inside

A complete fragment-shader curriculum wrapped in a game-shaped learning loop.

📚

100+ hand-written lesson pages

Every stage ships with an authored book covering the concept, the GLSL builtins involved, and a hint-driven challenge. EN + 한국어, fully searchable.

✅

Auto-grading at the press of a button

The Check button renders your shader and the reference solution at multiple time samples, then scores the per-channel difference. Tolerances are generous — no nitpicking over rounding.

⚡

Instant feedback, no toolchain

CodeMirror 6 editor, Three.js fullscreen quad, Ctrl+Enter to compile. Errors come back with named lines, not ERROR: 0:42.

🖼️

Bring your own texture

Upload an image, crop it square with built-in Cropper.js, and sample it in your shader via u_texture. Stages 6+ use it.

🌗

Light and dark themes

Cool-gray light palette for daylight, deep-purple dark mode for late-night shader hacking. Editor selection colors travel with the theme.

🌐

Runs in any modern browser

Built on WebGL via Three.js. No download, no install, no Node, no Unity. Open a tab and start writing shaders.
